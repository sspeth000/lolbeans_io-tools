LOLBeans runtime diagnostics — bookmarklet v2
==============================================

Your first report confirms that THREE and Ammo are global. This version performs a limited, read-only scan of objects reachable from window globals, looking for player/physics-related property names. It skips getters and does not change the game.

How to run:
1. Copy the entire one-line bookmarklet below.
2. Edit your browser bookmark and replace its URL/address field with the line.
3. Save, open https://lolbeans.io/, and tap the bookmark.
4. Copy the report JSON and send it back here.

Limitations:
- The scan is bounded to avoid walking the whole game runtime.
- Objects held only in private closures won't be discoverable from window globals.
- A candidate is only a clue, not proof that it is the active player object.
- Read-only diagnostics only; no movement or physics are modified.
